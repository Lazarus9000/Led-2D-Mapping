LED mapping viewer

Open a calibration file or drop it anywhere on this page.

Works with calibration.txt (x,y per line) and with the calibration.json written by calibrate_video.py, which also carries the detection quality of every LED. Drop the _reference.jpg as well to see the points on top of the camera picture.

Confidence threshold

0.50

LEDs below the threshold are not trusted. Their position is calculated from accepted LEDs instead.
